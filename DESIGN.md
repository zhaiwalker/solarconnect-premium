# Solarconnect redesign

Full visual redesign of the public homepage based on https://solarconnect.kz/, retrieved 8 September 2026. Source photos, contact information, equipment, installation steps and all twelve plan configurations come from the existing site. No customer testimonials or project outcomes were invented.

Direction: Apple-inspired product presentation for homeowners, businesses and agricultural customers in Kazakhstan. Centred product hero, dedicated studio renders, a restrained neutral palette, large Manrope typography retained from the original brand, editorial photography, readable descriptions and deliberate spaciousness. The user explicitly requested Apple-like design and parallel subagents for this second iteration.

Tokens: page #ffffff; surface #f5f5f7; ink #1d1d1f; muted #6e6e73; accent #23734e; borders #d9d9de. Pill actions, 20–28px media surfaces, 11px fields. Light default with a complete dark theme. Navigation alone uses translucent material. Primary body text 15–17px, secondary information 12–14px. Styles live in src/premium.css and src/energy-experience.css.

Motion: a single orchestrated hero entrance, subtle scroll-linked movement of the product, in-view statistics, critically damped tab indicators, responsive accordions and dialogs. Interactive Day / Evening / Backup scenes explain the role of panels and batteries with gradual lighting changes. No fabricated live data. Reduced motion, reduced transparency and increased contrast are supported. No scroll interception.

Contact forms prepare a WhatsApp message. The visitor explicitly opens and sends it; there is no simulated server submission. Prices and performance estimates are copied from source and marked as indicative. Articles and additional source pages remain linked to their existing URLs.

Product renders were generated with Imagegen as illustrations of solar technology, not photographs of a particular catalogue model. The remaining photography and company facts come from solarconnect.kz. Three subagents independently produced the studio asset, implemented the energy scene and audited usability. Apple Design, UI/UX Pro Max, Frontend Design, Impeccable and motion/usability guidance informed the design. Figma and unrelated plugins were not used because the deliverable is the working website.
